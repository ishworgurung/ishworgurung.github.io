🧠 *AI* · 💻 *Software development* · 🗄️ *Systems* · 🐧 *Linux/UNIX* · 📡 *Networking* · 🕸️ *Distributed systems*

I am an independent engineer working on AI infrastructure. I take on remote engineering work where I design and build the systems, not only run them.

The problems I enjoy most sit where several layers meet: networking, operating systems, software, AI workloads, GPUs and CPUs, and the Linux kernel. That is where performance, isolation and correctness are decided, and where a problem rarely stays inside one layer. The harder it is, the more I want to understand it. 🧩

In practice, that means building the layer that GPU and AI workloads run on: scheduling, isolation, networking and tracing, close to the hardware and the driver. I work alongside the kernel rather than inside it, mostly in Python, Go, Rust and eBPF, on a foundation of virtualization and security. 🐝

For ten years I built and ran production platforms at scale in Sydney. Before that, I designed a cloud API on top of OpenVZ, VMware ESX and XenServer, worked as a penetration tester in Sydney, and helped build a payment gateway for Brunei's Ministry of Finance. In my own time I study KVM, virtio and the NVIDIA driver interface. 📚

I am exacting where it matters: correctness, and the boundaries between components. Everywhere else, I choose the simplest design that meets the requirement, measure before I make a claim, and remove whatever does not earn its place. 🧮

The posts here are notes I write for myself, kept clear enough to be useful to others. Away from work, you will find me with a coffee, out on my KTM adventure bike, or with our daughters. 🏔️
